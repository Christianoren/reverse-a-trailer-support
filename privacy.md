# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

## Learn to Reverse a Trailer

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

---

## Data we collect

**Learn to Reverse a Trailer does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

- Sound preferences (on/off)
- Control layout preference
- Level completion progress

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

---

## Third-party services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

---

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
